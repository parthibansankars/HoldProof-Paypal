# Demo video script (target 2:50)

One continuous take where possible. Two phones: the worker's phone runs HoldProof; the client's phone scans the QR code. Show the PayPal Sandbox merchant activity page in a browser at the end. Use only real sandbox transactions recorded during the take.

| Time | Picture | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Worker at a cluttered garage corner, client beside them | "A solo worker, a stranger's garage, a sixty-dollar job. The client won't pay a stranger up front. The worker won't work for nothing. HoldProof fixes the first minute and the last." |
| 0:15–0:40 | New job → "Use the demo job" → criteria appear; "good quality" in the refused list | "The worker describes the job. Our Proposer agent turns it into criteria a photo can prove, and refuses the ones it can't." |
| 0:40–1:05 | BEFORE photo → digest shown → QR code; client scans and approves with a PayPal Sandbox account; status flips to HELD | "A BEFORE photo seals the agreement into a fingerprint, written into the PayPal order before the client approves. The client approves a PayPal hold: the money is committed, but not moved." |
| 1:05–1:40 | Corner cleared → AFTER photo → Proposer ticks criteria → Critic: no objection → Judge: 11 green checks → Release → PayPal capture COMPLETED | "Two AI agents check the work. The Proposer proposes; the Critic tries to prove it wrong. Then the Judge, plain code with no AI, is the only thing allowed to touch PayPal. Every check passes, the worker confirms, and PayPal confirms the capture." |
| 1:40–2:10 | Second job: AFTER photo of a different wall → Critic objects "different scene" → Judge refuses → "no PayPal call" → hold voided | "Here's what happens when someone cheats. A photo of somewhere else: the Critic objects, the Judge refuses, and no money moves. The hold goes back to the client." |
| 2:10–2:30 | Verify record: recomputed digest = custom_id read from PayPal → PDF export | "Weeks later, if there's a dispute, the record proves nothing changed after the client approved, because the proof lives in PayPal itself." |
| 2:30–2:45 | Sandbox merchant page showing the capture and the voided authorization | "Every state you saw came from PayPal: orders, authorizations, captures, voids and refunds, all in the sandbox." |
| 2:45–2:50 | HoldProof icon, tagline | "HoldProof. A hold you can see, released by proof you can check. Built for the PayPal AI Hackathon, sandbox only." |

Rules for recording: no statistics; no claims of escrow or arbitration; show the "PayPal Sandbox · Hackathon demo" badge in frame at least once.
